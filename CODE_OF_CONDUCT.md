# Community conduct

Be respectful, factual and constructive. Discuss evidence and software behavior
without attacking contributors, SAR teams, or people involved in incidents.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual content directed at community
members, spam and deliberate misinformation are not welcome. Do not speculate
about victims or publish unnecessary personal details, private medical records,
contact information or identifying details about minors.

Daniel (@dgriff03) maintains this project and may remove inappropriate content,
close submissions or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate
to the behavior and its impact. This applies in issues, pull requests and other
project-controlled discussions.

Flag inappropriate GitHub content using GitHub's reporting controls. For ordinary
project conduct concerns, notify the maintainer without reposting sensitive
material. Do not use public issues to disclose private information or secrets.
